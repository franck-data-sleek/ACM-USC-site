## the-problem

Small and mid-sized businesses in insurance, construction, healthcare and transportation can't compete with big tech for engineering talent. At the same time, most student projects never touch a real business process.

## how-it-works

  1. 01
    A company submits a problem

    A real workflow it wants to improve with AI or data.

  2. 02
    A student team scopes and builds it

    A project lead plus [N] students, one semester, with mentors.

  3. 03
    Demo Day

    The company gets a working prototype; students get a real case study for their portfolio.
